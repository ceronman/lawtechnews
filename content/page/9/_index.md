## 8 July 2026

### [EU General Court dismisses Apple's challenge to its gatekeeper designation under the DMA](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2026-07/cp260096en.pdf)

<span class="news-item__source">curia.europa.eu</span> The General Court of the EU (Cases T-1079/23, T-1080/23, T-214/24) rejected all of Apple's actions against its designation as a "gatekeeper" for the App Store and iOS under the Digital Markets Act, confirming that Apple's multiple App Store versions count as a single core platform service. Apple must now await future Commission enforcement actions; a significant DMA case-law precedent for platform-regulation practice.

### [EDPB sheds light on anonymisation and web scraping for generative AI and adopts final version of guidelines on blockchain](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-sheds-light-on-anonymisation-and-web-scraping-for-generative-ai-and-adopts-final-version_en)

<span class="news-item__source">edpb.europa.eu</span> The EDPB adopted new draft guidelines clarifying when data qualifies as "anonymous" under the GDPR (introducing a three-part test: no record isolation, no linkage, no inference), alongside guidelines on the GDPR treatment of web scraping for generative AI training (legal basis, transparency, special-category data) and the final version of its blockchain guidelines. Directly relevant to GDPR/AI Act compliance work; open for public consultation until 30 October 2026.

### [AMLA introduces a common EU approach to enforcing anti-money laundering rules](https://www.amla.europa.eu/press-release-amla-introduces-common-eu-approach-enforcing-anti-money-laundering-rules_en)

<span class="news-item__source">amla.europa.eu</span> The EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority published final draft Regulatory Technical Standards (under Article 53(10) AMLD) requiring supervisors across all Member States to use a shared, four-tier method for grading the gravity of AML/CFT breaches and determining enforcement outcomes — covering both financial and non-financial sectors. Once adopted by the Commission, the standards become directly binding EU-wide. Squarely in Maria's AML/KYC compliance specialization.

### [AI increases the risks of cyberattacks](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en/current/ai-increases-the-risks-of-cyberattacks)

<span class="news-item__source">autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl</span> The Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) warned, in its 2025 annual data-breach report, that AI is fueling a "flywheel" of increasingly realistic phishing and a near-tripling of account-takeover incidents (607 in 2024 to 1,742 in 2025), and called on organisations' boards and management to treat digital security as a top-level priority. Directly relevant given the AP is one of Maria's primary target employers, and the report sits exactly at the intersection of her GDPR/data-breach and cybersecurity-regulation expertise.

## 7 July 2026

### [Commission refers Ireland, Spain, France and the Netherlands to the Court of Justice for failing to transpose NIS2](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1499)

<span class="news-item__source">ec.europa.eu</span> The European Commission filed legal referrals at the CJEU against Ireland, Spain, France and the Netherlands for being more than 20 months late transposing the NIS2 cybersecurity directive into national law, risking financial penalties. Directly relevant given Maria's Netherlands focus and interest in cybersecurity regulation and EU enforcement mechanisms.

## 6 July 2026

### [Joint Statement: Pegasus in the European Parliament, the EU Must Act Now](https://edri.org/our-work/joint-statement-pegasus-in-the-european-parliament-the-eu-must-act-now/)

<span class="news-item__source">edri.org</span> A Citizen Lab forensic analysis found that former MEP Stelios Kouloglou — while serving on the European Parliament's own committee investigating Pegasus spyware abuse (PEGA) — was himself hacked with Pegasus spyware in 2022 and 2023. EDRi and other civil society groups issued a joint statement calling it a "rule of law emergency" and demanding an independent DG ITEC investigation, a Commission response to the PEGA Committee's recommendations, stronger enforcement of the EU's dual-use export regulation, and a ban on EU funds supporting spyware companies. Primary source: [Citizen Lab report](https://citizenlab.ca/research/member-of-committee-investigating-spyware-hacked-with-pegasus/). Directly relevant to Maria's digital-rights, EU-institutions, and human-rights specialization.

## 1 July 2026

### [Trump's court win reignites fight to sink €1.7T data deal with Europe](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/meetings/122nd-plenary-meeting_en)

<span class="news-item__source">edpb.europa.eu</span> Following the US Supreme Court's Trump v. Slaughter ruling expanding the president's power to fire independent agency commissioners, European privacy regulators and activists (including Max Schrems/Noyb) are moving to challenge the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, arguing it undermines the independent-oversight guarantees the deal depends on. The EDPB placed the ruling on its 7 July plenary agenda to assess implications for the Framework's oversight mechanisms, while the Commission says it is "carefully analysing" the ruling. A potential third collapse of an EU-US data transfer deal (after Safe Harbor and Privacy Shield) — core to Maria's GDPR/international-data-transfer specialization.

### [EDPB and AMLA to develop Joint Guidelines on partnerships for information sharing](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-and-amla-to-develop-joint-guidelines-on-partnerships-for-information-sharing_en)

<span class="news-item__source">edpb.europa.eu</span> The European Data Protection Board and the new EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA) announced they will jointly develop guidelines on how companies, supervisors, FIUs and data protection authorities can share information to fight financial crime while complying with the GDPR, under Article 75 of the AML Regulation (applicable from 10 July 2027). A stakeholder event and public consultation are planned for 2027. A notable early signal of exactly the AML/KYC–data protection intersection in Maria's profile.

## 29 June 2026

### [Artificial Intelligence: Council gives final green light to simplify and streamline rules](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/06/29/artificial-intelligence-council-gives-final-green-light-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)

<span class="news-item__source">consilium.europa.eu</span> The Council formally adopted the "Digital Omnibus on AI," closing the legislative procedure that amends the EU AI Act. Key changes: high-risk AI safety-compliance deadlines pushed to December 2027 (standalone) and August 2028 (embedded AI); AI-generated content labelling obligations starting August 2026; a ban on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM from December 2026; and AI regulatory sandboxes delayed to August 2027. A major recalibration of the AI Act's implementation timeline, core to Maria's AI Act specialization.
